# Shoreline v17 — adaptive DPR for slower graphics processors
Date: 2026-10-22

## Goal
Maintain the clarity of the existing two 3D breakers and wet-beach wash on capable GPUs while preventing sustained low frame rates on older integrated graphics. The previous DPR logic only reduced quality below 23 FPS and could recover too quickly.

## Implementation
- Add pure bounded `createAdaptiveResolution` controller with explicit conditions for resizing:
  - Below 36 FPS for at least 6 visible seconds: reduce DPR by 10%, no lower than 0.85.
  - Above 56 FPS for at least 18 visible seconds: restore DPR by 8%, never above the initial device cap.
  - 12 visible seconds of cooldown after a change, a neutral FPS band, and minimum .025 DPR difference prevent oscillation.
  - Hidden tabs, invalid samples, and long wake-from-sleep stalls do not drive resizing.
- Integrate controller into the existing 1-second FPS calculation. Keep wave geometry, shaders, birds, sounds, save data and editor features unchanged.
- Existing opt-in `?perf=1` still displays 12-second mean RAF FPS, p95/p99 frame intervals and actual DPR.
