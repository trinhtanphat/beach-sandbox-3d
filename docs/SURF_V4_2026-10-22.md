# Surf v4 visual acceptance — 2026-10-22

## What was wrong
Previous surf used narrow sine-driven foam stripes that were not locked to the actual displaced wave crest. The water plane was set at Y=0.047, below the coast terrain near Y=0.08, so breakers disappeared under the beach. The original beach swash lacked convincing retreat.

## Corrections
- Water plane raised to Y=0.104 so shoreline shallow water is not buried by the sand.
- Ocean vertex elevation and foam shading now share the same pair of shoreward-moving fronts (about 4.35 seconds per cycle), with staggered crests, narrower solid white breaking lips and a dissipating turbulent tail.
- Breaking crests now concentrate within the first ~34% of the nearshore water band rather than forming noisy white stripes across the deep ocean.
- Beach swash spans coast minus 1.35 to coast plus 0.80 world units with 220 by 32 mesh tessellation. A leading white rim travels up the sand and retreats, with gently oscillating residual foamy lace along the coast.
- Spray is a single batched points object, 460 deterministic foam flecks following the same swell front.
- Caustic strength has been softened to keep clear blue/teal water visible instead of dense white noise.
- Terrain sculpting updates the swash mesh height.
