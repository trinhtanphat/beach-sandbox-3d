# Shoreline Surf v12 — turbulent foam wake and timed shorewash

**Release candidate:** 2026-10-22
**Repository:** trinhtanphat/beach-sandbox-3d
**Branch:** feat/surf-v12-turbulent-wake
**Production target:** https://beach.qs3d.site/

## Why

In v11, the two raised 3D breakers were synchronized with the whitewater and foam particles, but the foam behind a crest was still a relatively uniform band. The shoreline also kept a thin white stripe between impacts, and the wet-sand layer looked too teal.

## Changes

- A new **turbulent wake** profile rises immediately behind the rolling wave, breaks into advected cellular bubbles and filaments, and dissipates within roughly 0.185 ocean UV units. Its deterministic JS analogue `trailingWake()` is covered by range/dissipation tests.
- The foam pattern now follows shoreward flow via animated noise coordinates instead of independent static sine stripes. Both existing phased wave trains retain the same ocean crest and GPU raised lip mechanics.
- A narrow shaded trough on the **seaward side** of each crest adds contrast and perceived wave height without expensive post-processing.
- The shoreline's former near-permanent white stripe was replaced by an **impact-timed pulse** for both wave trains, based on the wave phase; the CPU `shorewashPulse()` matches the GLSL phase profile and repeats smoothly.
- Thin water over retreating sand uses a neutral warm dark-sand tone rather than a turquoise tint. Wet sand remains independent from the Whitewater foam slider.
