# Surf v13 — backwash that exposes wet sand on the landward side
Date: 2026-10-22

## Problem addressed

The v12 beach shader shaded the thin wet film primarily on the seaward side of the moving surf front. When a wave retreats, however, the exposed wet-sand band lies *behind the receding waterline*, inland of the current water's edge, between its maximum run-up and its current position. This had made water retreat look visually disconnected from the sand.

## Implementation

- Added deterministic CPU reference `exposedSandBand(time, alongShore, x, strength)` for the previously covered, newly exposed sand. It uses the same swash phase and run-up equations as the fragment shader.
- In the swash GLSL, measure `maximumReach=.84-run` and the active front. The wet-sand strip is bounded by maximum reach at its inland end and the retreating shoreline at its seaward end. It activates *only while withdrawing*.
- Exposed sand fades by phase .99, before the next wave begins. A thin clear-water film remains on the water side of the active breaker.
- Added small, irregular backwash filaments advancing seaward as the wave drains; low-intensity and driven by the Whitewater foam control. The wet-sand layer stays independent of foam intensity.
- Only `src/waves.js` shader/CPU reference and tests were changed, with no new textures, postprocessing passes, GPU draw calls, DNS or persistence schema changes.
