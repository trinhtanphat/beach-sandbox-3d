Welcome to Shoreline.

It's a tiny ocean you can play with. Waves wash along a procedurally generated beach as the sun travels overhead.

  • Drag the scene to orbit; scroll to zoom.
  • Select Build sand, Dig or Rake and drag on terrain.
  • Plant trees, scatter rocks or launch boats.
  • Pause the clock, dial up wave energy, or adjust the sun using the time dial.
  • Press Sound to hear generated surf and occasional seabird calls. Sound starts only after your click.
  • Use keys 1–8 to change tools; Space to pause.
